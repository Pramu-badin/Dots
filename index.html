Our Story

Founded in 2018, Brown Dots Coffee Shop began with a simple mission: to serve exceptional coffee in a warm, welcoming environment. Our name comes from the beautiful brown dots that appear on perfectly roasted coffee beans.

We source our beans directly from sustainable farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, ensuring every cup tells a story of quality and care.
